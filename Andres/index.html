Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a full detail take?

Typically, a full detail takes between 3 to 5 hours depending on the condition of the vehicle. Paint correction packages can take a full day.

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

No! Our mobile vans are completely self-sufficient with their own spot-free water tank and generator power. We can wash your car anywhere.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, cash, Apple Pay, and Google Pay upon completion of the service.
