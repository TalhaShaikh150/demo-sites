Common Questions

Client Information

Do I need to provide water or power?

No. Our mobile unit is fully autonomous. We carry our own spot-free water tank and generator power. We can service your vehicle at your apartment complex, office garage, or driveway without needing any connections.

How long does a Paint Correction take?

This depends on the level of correction required. A 1-step polish typically takes 4-6 hours. A multi-stage correction for showroom perfection can take 1-2 days. We prioritize quality over speed.

What areas do you service?

We cover the majority of South Florida, specifically Miami-Dade and Broward County. This includes Brickell, Coral Gables, Miami Beach, Fort Lauderdale, and Weston.

How do I maintain my Ceramic Coating?

We recommend a bi-weekly maintenance wash using pH-neutral soaps. Avoid automatic car washes with brushes, as they will damage the coating's hydrophobic properties. We offer maintenance plans specifically for coated vehicles.
